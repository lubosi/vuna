# Glossary

**MOQ** — Minimum order quantity. Seller-set, per listing, in the product's canonical unit.

**RFQ** — Request for quote. A buyer's inquiry against a listing; negotiated over WhatsApp, not in-app.

**Corridor** — A growing region paired with a buyer city/market. Launch corridor: Lusaka peri-urban growing areas → Lusaka.

**Canonical unit** — The single unit (`kg`, `tonne`, `litre`, or `stem`) a catalogue product is always sold in. Owned by the product, not the listing.

**Grade** — A quality tier from a controlled per-product vocabulary (e.g. "Grade A", "Export").

**Alias** — A local-language or synonym search term attached to a catalogue product, so buyers can find it without knowing its canonical name.

**pending_review** — A listing status: submitted, not yet checked by ops, not publicly visible.

**Off-platform** — Payment and delivery, which happen outside Vuna in v1; the app only facilitates discovery and introduction.
